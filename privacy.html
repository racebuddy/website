Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how RaceBuddy ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or take part in an event that uses RaceBuddy.

Who is responsible for your data

When you contact us through this website, or when an event organiser or their staff use a RaceBuddy account, we are responsible for that information.

When you apply for or take part in an event that uses RaceBuddy, the event organiser decides what information is collected and how it is used. We process it on their behalf. Please read the organiser's own privacy policy too. They are your first point of contact for questions about your event data.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Event organisers and staff: account details such as your name and email address, and records of actions taken in the software.
  • Event participants: the information requested by the event organiser. This typically includes contact details, personal details needed for entry and results, payment records, documents you upload, and event progress such as tracking, checkpoint and finishing data.
  • Technical data: limited information such as IP address, browser type and error logs, used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and provide support.
  • To provide and run the RaceBuddy software for event organisers.
  • To manage event applications, payments, refunds, tracking and results on behalf of event organisers.
  • To send emails related to an event or your account.
  • To maintain security, fix problems and improve the service.

We rely on performing a contract, our legitimate interests in running and improving the service, legal obligations and, where needed, your consent. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to help run RaceBuddy, for example for hosting, storage, email delivery, payment processing, authentication, error monitoring and live tracking. They only process data on our instructions and under appropriate agreements. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Some event information, such as names, progress and results, may be shown publicly by the event organiser.

International transfers

Our data is mainly stored in the UK. Some service providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as agreed with the event organiser, and to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts and styles are loaded from third-party content delivery networks, which may receive your IP address. The RaceBuddy application uses only the storage it needs to sign you in and work properly.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to receive a copy of it. For event data, please contact the event organiser first. We will help them respond. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email hello@racebuddy.co.uk.
